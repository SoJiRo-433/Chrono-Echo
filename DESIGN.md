## Logline

In a world where science has shattered the boundaries between reality and fantasy, the prestigious
**Aetherion Institute** stands as humanity's crown jewel of temporal and spatial engineering. At the
heart of its most ambitious endeavour was **Aionia Avgerinos**, a brilliant yet nonchalant student
whose mastery of chronology surpassed even her seasoned peers. She uncovers a horrifying truth in
the data - every timeline in existence is spiralling toward total collapse and death - and resolves
to erase herself from all of them. When her partner learns of it, he refuses to let her vanish, and
his counter-initiative tears time apart. Aionia wakes with no memory, in the ruins, and has to
decide whether to finish her mission or stay.

## The world

The Aetherion Institute is the setting for the whole game: a campus of laboratories, corridors and
dormitories dedicated to studying time and space. After the disaster it is dormant and ruined -
scattered papers, broken machines, broken glass, emergency lights flickering, the hum of dying
machinery. Later areas are overgrown and shattered, and the corridors are where the scale of the
timeline collapse becomes visible.

## Backstory

- **Project Hyperion** - the flagship project. A machine designed to calculate and traverse
  space-time trajectories, led by Aionia Avgerinos and her partner Felix Lasarewitch.
- **The truth in the data** - Aionia finds that this timeline, and the trajectory the project is
  building toward, leads to disaster and death for all timelines. She keeps it to herself.
- **Her plan** - use Hyperion to reverse every timeline in which she does not exist, erasing herself
  everywhere, on the belief that her death is the only variable that can save the multiverse.
- **Project Theia** - Felix learns what she is doing and builds a counter-initiative to bind her to
  reality regardless of the cost, and to stop whatever she is doing.
- **The sabotage** - rewriting the laws of temporal mechanics invites catastrophe. A mysterious
  sabotage ignites an explosion, leaving fatalities and fracturing the fabric of time itself. Who
  sabotaged it is still an open question in the document.
- **The aftermath** - the collapse does not destroy reality; it forces every parallel timeline to
  collide and merge into one unstable present. Everyone Aionia once knew is now present at once,
  each from a different timeline.
- **Where the game starts** - Aionia wakes in a deserted, ruined room of the institute, her mind
  wiped blank by the temporal backlash. She must collect the **memory fragments** Felix hid across
  the campus - deliberately, because he is obsessed with her and has frozen his own world until she
  remembers him.

## Lore: the Law of Temporal Displacement

In the physics of the Aetherion Institute, moving or interacting across fractured timelines requires
a stable reference point - otherwise a person's physical form would dissolve into the temporal void.

**The pendant as a temporal anchor.** Disguised as a remote controller, the device locks Aionia's
spatial coordinates to a single "present", preventing her from being pulled apart by the collapsing
timelines. Felix gave it to her as a "physical failsafe" for the Hyperion core mainframe, with an
override frequency to anchor her position and recalibrate the system if the spatial trajectory
fluctuated.

**How the Echo works.** When Aionia activates the Echo mechanism, the pendant does not clone her. It
exploits a localized micro-loop in the time trajectory: it "unpins" a few seconds of her immediate
past and projects a translucent copy of her past actions into the room. Because time inside the
facility is stuttering and overlapping, this self-projection acts as a bridge across time itself -
forcing her past self to repeat an action (like holding a pressure switch) turns temporal distortion
into her path forward.

## Demo / tutorial beats

The document scripts the opening as eight scenes in the ruined room. These map onto the two demo
rooms in this repo.

1. **Waking Up** - Aionia wakes alone in a dormant, ruined room of the Aetherion Institute. Basic
   movement tutorial (W/A/S/D) through the debris. Dialogue: "W-what happened? Where am I?" Narrator:
   the space looks abandoned; find out what happened.
2. **Exploring the Room** - object interaction with **E**. A dusty report log, and a half-burned
   photo of her and another person facing away. Dialogue: "Project Hyperion, to traverse time and
   space... Interesting." / "That looks like me, and someone else... But why do I not remember who
   that is?" / "I should keep this."
3. **Looking Around** (extension of scene 2) - inspect at least three objects to trigger her internal
   monologue before the path forward opens: check boxes ("Just some mechanical parts."), the
   destroyed machine ("Probably the machine that is supposed to traverse time."), photos, drawers.
4. **Discovering the Mysterious Pendant** - a pendant on the floor, pulsing with a faint, unnatural
   blue glow. Collecting it adds it to her inventory. Dialogue: "What is this?"
5. **Interacting with the Pendant** - the glow intensifies with a chime and a visual distortion, and
   she gets a flash of Felix giving her the pendant as a "physical failsafe" for the Hyperion core,
   with an override frequency to anchor her position. She gets a headache: "Argh! What just
   happened?!" / "Who is that person who gave me this?" The pendant becomes her key item and
   introduces the Echo ability.
6. **First Echo Tutorial** - the pendant reacts to a spatial anomaly and casts a translucent, glowing
   blue "Echo" of Aionia that repeats her recent movements step-for-step. On-screen instructions
   teach the core mechanic: press **R** to record a short action window, move around, then stop
   recording so the Echo loops those exact movements. Dialogue: "Wha... It copies my movements?" /
   "I need to test this out." / "Hah... Amazing" / "I can use this to get out of here."
7. **Tutorial Puzzle (the Pressure Switch)** - the exit is blocked by a heavy security door powered
   down by the blackout, alongside a floor pressure switch that needs continuous weight to keep the
   circuit open. Aionia cannot be in two places at once: record her stepping onto and holding the
   switch, and once the recording stops the Echo stands on it while the live Aionia walks through.
8. **Leaving the Room** - she passes through the door into the overgrown, shattered corridors of the
   institute. The scale of the timeline collapse hits her, and she realises the pendant is
   intimately tied to her lost memories. The tutorial ends and the main exploration/puzzle adventure
   begins.

## Endings

The document lists ending branches without full scripts yet.

**Main endings**

- **True Ending** - requires having romanced Felix and completed the game once. Aionia and the others
  are fine; the world is reset. The realities are changed so that Aionia and Felix are safe, and they
  forget what happened for good.
- **Neutral Good Ending** - complete a game and fix the timeline. Aionia is safe.
- **Sacrificial Ending** - Aionia sacrifices herself (goes through with her plan). Felix is
  distraught; he only remembers her.
- **No More Time Ending** - failed to remember (lost fragments? how?). Aionia failed to figure out
  what really happened and stayed lost.

**Romance endings**

- **Yoruka (good)** - convinced Yoruka to heal herself, managed to remember, fixed the timeline.
- **Yoruka (bad)** - she became a yandere, the timeline was not fixed, Yoruka killed you.
- **Yoruka (alternate)** - yandere Yoruka, but the timeline is fixed because she kills Felix instead.
- **Adellaine (good)** - fixed the timeline; you and Adellaine lived a happy life together (she became
  a veterinarian instead).
- **Adellaine (bad)** - did not fix the timeline; Adellaine lost trust in you, left you alone, and
  destroyed all the fragments, leaving you dead.
- **Hesiod (good)** - fixed the timeline; you and Hesiod enjoy life together and take care of the
  company.
- **Hesiod (bad)** - did not fix the timeline; he used the fragments and sacrificed you.
- **Felix (bad, together)** - sacrificed the two of you.
- **Felix (bad)** - fed his delusions while the timeline is fixed.
- **Felix (good)** - fixed the timeline and talked him out of it.
